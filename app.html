How to Choose the Right Editing App

01

Check editing features: Ensure the app supports keyframing, masking, or AI generation if your workflow requires it.

02

Check device compatibility: Some applications require high-end processing power for rendering.

03

Check export quality: Verify if the app allows 4K or 1080p 60fps exports without aggressive watermarks.

04

Use authorized sources: Always download tools from verified distribution channels to maintain security.
