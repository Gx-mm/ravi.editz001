Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ravi EDITZ handles information when visitors access and use our website (https://ravieditz-ai.vercel.app/).

We are committed to maintaining transparency regarding the collection, processing, and protection of data related to your use of our AI creative platform.

2

Information We Collect

Visitors can freely browse Ravi EDITZ without creating an account or logging in. We do not intentionally request personal information merely for browsing the website.

However, if you voluntarily choose to contact us by email regarding questions, feedback, or support, any information you include in that communication (such as your email address and the content of your message) will be collected solely to respond to your inquiry.

3

Automatically Collected Technical Information

Depending on the services used to host, secure, or deliver the website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by those service providers. This may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information and operating system
  • Request information (such as pages visited and time of access)
  • Approximate technical or log information

This data is generally processed at the infrastructure level to ensure the website functions correctly, remains secure, and performs optimally.

4

How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided through email communication may be used to:

  • Respond to your questions or feedback.
  • Investigate reported technical issues.
  • Address copyright concerns or content corrections.
  • Improve website-related services based on user suggestions.
5

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small data files placed on your device. The website may use cookies or similar technologies if required by certain services used on the platform (such as essential hosting or security mechanisms).

If advertising or analytics services are introduced in the future, those specific third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own independent privacy policies to function properly.

6

Google AdSense and Advertising

Ravi EDITZ may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdSense, in the future to support the platform.

If Google advertising is implemented, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve, measure, or personalize advertising, subject to applicable policies and user choices.

To learn more about how Google uses data when you use partners' sites or apps, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7

Third-Party Services

We may utilize third-party services to host, secure, deliver, analyze, or advertise on the website. These external service providers process technical information according to their own respective privacy policies.

8

Third-Party Links

Ravi EDITZ may provide links to external websites, AI generators, or services for creative and educational purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of these external websites.

Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policies of any external services they choose to visit.

9

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to maintain the security of our website. However, please be aware that no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10

Children's Privacy

Ravi EDITZ does not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children through a registration or account system. The platform is designed for a general audience seeking creative AI resources.

11

Your Choices

Visitors may choose not to voluntarily provide personal information by simply choosing not to contact us via email. If cookies are utilized by third-party services (such as future advertising or analytics), users may have controls available through their specific browser settings or the relevant service providers.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically when:

  • Website functionality changes.
  • Third-party services or infrastructure change.
  • Advertising integrations are introduced.
  • Legal or policy requirements evolve.

Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
